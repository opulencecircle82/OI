ABOUT US

Affordable A320 practice software for aviation schools

OI Aviation makes realistic A320 practice apps that a school buys once and uses for good: on a tablet or PC, in the classroom or at home, with no yearly fees.

Why we started

OI Aviation began with an A320 Flight Attendant Panel simulator for an aviation college in the Philippines. Students got very little time on real trainers. Most training software is sold as a yearly subscription, often per student. Many schools can't afford that.

So we built our own practice tools. They follow the real A320. They're easy to set up in a lab. And they're priced for schools. Today there are four apps for pilots and cabin crew: A320 MCDU Trainer, A320 FAP Simulator, A320 Cabin Safety and Cabin Entertainment.
