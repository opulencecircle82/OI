A320 MCDU Trainer · OI Aviation
AIRBUS A320 · MCDU · ND · WXR
A320 MCDU Trainer
Program the A320 MCDU, watch the flight on the Navigation Display and learn to read the weather radar. Then fly the route and see the waypoints sequence.
DOWNLOAD APK (https://github.com/opulencecircle82/OI/releases/download/mcdu-v2.3/A320_MCDU_Trainer_v2.3.apk) DOWNLOAD FOR WINDOWS (https://github.com/opulencecircle82/OI/releases/download/mcdu-v2.3/A320_MCDU_Trainer_Setup_v2.3.exe) WATCH TUTORIAL
v 2.3 · Android 7.0+ tablet or phone · Windows 10 / 11 PC (64-bit)
Cold & dark to shutdown
Overhead panel with BAT 1 / 2, EXT PWR, APU MASTER SW, START and BLEED, the three ADIRS selectors with ON BATT and ALIGN lights, and the engine MODE and MASTER switches. Align the IRS on IRS INIT, start the engines, fly, then shut down.
Departure & arrival
DEPARTURE and ARRIVAL pages with real runways and fictional training SIDs, STARs, ILS and RNAV approaches; airways, holds, offsets, FIX INFO, step climbs, wind pages and the SEC F-PLN.
Approach & go-around
The approach phase starts at (DECEL), or by hand with ACTIVATE APPR PHASE and CONFIRM. TOGA flies the missed approach with the approach strung again; then a second approach, or ENABLE ALTN to divert. PERF APPR, PERF GO AROUND and the IRS check on POSITION MONITOR.
MCDU like the aircraft
14-line display with the Airbus colour code, line select keys, scratchpad and CLR logic, and real messages such as NOT ALLOWED and FORMAT ERROR.
Navigation Display
Mode and range selectors as on the EFIS panel: ROSE ILS / VOR / NAV, ARC and PLAN, 10 to 320 NM, CSTR / WPT / VOR.D / NDB / ARPT, and the temporary flight plan in yellow.
Weather radar
MultiScan panel laid out as in the cockpit: GAIN and TILT knobs, the MODE selector (WX, WX+T, TURB, MAP) and the SYS, MULTISCAN, GCS and PWS switches. A high tilt overshoots the storm; a low tilt paints the ground.
TRAINER
Press FLY to fly your flight plan from Manila (RPLL) to Cebu (RPVM) at ×1, ×4 or ×16 speed. Waypoints sequence on the F-PLN page, a discontinuity stops managed navigation, TOGA starts a go-around in the approach phase, and RESET brings the aircraft back to the gate. Dashed boxes mark controls that exist only in the trainer.
FAULTS lets the instructor fail a system: FM1 or FM2 (single FMGC, PLEASE WAIT after the reset), IND, MCDU FAIL, GPS (GPS PRIMARY LOST, NAV ACCUR DOWNGRAD on the approach), weather radar system 1 or 2, VOR 1, ILS, and IRS DRIFT (IRS more than 5 NM from the FMGC position: fast alignment).
TUTORIAL
A complete flight, cold and dark to shutdown
A complete flight, Manila to Cebu: cold and dark, batteries, ground power, ADIRS and APU, INIT A, IRS INIT and the IRS check, departure and arrival, INIT B and PERF, engine start, take-off, weather radar, descent preparation, the approach phase, a go-around and a second approach, landing, the CFDS report and the shutdown.

MCDU PAGES
The pages a student uses first
White is for titles and prompts, blue for data you can change, green for active data, amber boxes for required entries, magenta for constraints and yellow for a temporary flight plan.
INIT A / B
FROM/TO, company route, ALTN, flight number, cost index, CRZ FL, weights and fuel.
F-PLN
Insert and clear waypoints, discontinuities, speed and altitude constraints, (T/C) and (T/D).
LAT REV / VERT REV
NEXT WPT and NEW DEST with a yellow TMPY plan: *ERASE or INSERT*.
DIR TO
Direct to any point from the present position, then INSERT.
PERF
TAKE OFF V1 / VR / V2 with the disagree check, FLAPS/THS, FLEX; CLB, CRZ (DES CABIN RATE), DES, APPR and GO AROUND; ACTIVATE APPR PHASE.
PROG
Cruise level, optimum and maximum level, bearing and distance to any point.
RAD NAV
VOR, ILS and ADF by identifier or frequency, with course.
FUEL PRED / DATA
Fuel at destination and alternate, extra fuel, POSITION MONITOR with the IRS distance from the FMGC position, aircraft status.
DEPARTURE / ARRIVAL
Runway, SID and transition; approach, STAR and VIA; yellow TMPY, INSERT; the missed approach, flown after TOGA; ENABLE ALTN.
IRS INIT / WIND / SEC
ALIGN ON REF and CONFIRM ALIGN, CLIMB / CRUISE / DESCENT WIND with WIND REQUEST, INIT REQUEST, SEC F-PLN.
HOLD / OFFSET / AIRWAYS
Holding pattern with IMM EXIT, lateral offset, airway VIA / TO, FIX INFO, LL XING, PLACE/BRG/DIST waypoints.
MCDU MENU / CFDS
Maintenance menu: leg reports and AVIONICS STATUS from the trainer faults, SYSTEM REPORT / TEST, CIDS 1 and 2 reports, LRU IDENT, GROUND SCANNING, SYSTEM CONFIG, OP TESTS and system tests.
LAT REV: the new waypoint shows as a yellow temporary flight plan
Manual tilt too low: ground returns over the islands
DIR TO from the present position
PLAN mode with the WPT option
Power-up: batteries, EXT PWR, APU starting, ADIRS aligning (HDG / MAP NOT AVAIL on the ND)
ARRIVAL TO RPVM: ILS22 and the training STARs
Go-around: the missed approach to VM22M, then the approach again
Trainer faults: FM1 failed, GPS lost, weather radar system 1 failed
CFDS: CIDS 1 SYSTEM TESTS from SYSTEM REPORT / TEST, COM
CFDS LAST LEG REPORT: the faults of the flight with UTC and ATA

EXAM with a score at the end
Ten shuffled items, placed in any order on any of the 17 locations, with no hints. After SUBMIT: the score, the percentage and every answer with the correct place.
