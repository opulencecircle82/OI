A320 Cabin Trainer

Learn where the emergency equipment lives on the A320, prove it with a drag-and-drop quiz, and walk through the cabin entertainment system on a simulated seat-back screen.

SEE WHAT IT DOES

v1.0 · Android 7.0+ tablet · Windows 10 / 11 PC (64-bit) · pilot deployment — ask your instructor for the build

FEATURES

Two modules, one cabin

Emergency Equipment for the safety briefing and the test, Cabin Entertainment for the system walkthrough and the passenger demo.

Interactive cabin map

A top-view A320 schematic with every emergency-equipment item placed in its zone: extinguishers, PBE, life vests, slide/rafts, megaphones, first-aid kits, torches and signalling devices. Filter by category, tap a marker and read what it is, how many are fitted and where they live.

Drag-and-drop quiz

Each round picks eight items from the catalog. Students drag an equipment chip onto the matching location on the cabin map; the answer is revealed on the map itself and the best score is saved on the device.

IFE system diagram

The cabin entertainment architecture as a static schematic for briefing: IFE Control / RCC, IFEC, ADB, FDB, SEB and the SDUs, with the CIDS branch — the same signal path the student sees on the demo screen.

Passenger demo

A simulated seat-back screen: Movies, TV & Safety, Music channels, Flight Info with a moving map of the MNL–Cebu route, and Service for call and reading-light controls. Fully offline demo content, clearly marked DEMO.

Instructor override

A PIN-protected instructor panel takes over the demo: the PA announcement mutes the music channel while it plays, and best scores can be reset between classes.

Built for the classroom tablet

Landscape layout sized for a 10-inch tablet, touch targets of 24 dp and more, and everything runs offline — no accounts, no internet, nothing to configure between sessions.

SCREENS

What the student sees

Every screen runs offline on the classroom tablet. The instructor sits beside the student and drives the demo.
