SECTION 01

Purpose and How to Read This Report

This report lists every function of the A320 FAP Simulator, the source it was built from, and how closely it follows the real Airbus A320 Cabin Intercommunication Data System (CIDS) Flight Attendant Panel (FAP).

Status labels

LabelMeaning
MATCHESBehaves like the real A320 FAP, confirmed by at least one technical or training source.
SIMPLIFIEDThe function exists on the real FAP, but the simulator shows a simplified or typical version (for example a fixed list, fewer options, or values that differ between airlines).
TRAINERNot a FAP function. Dashed TRAINER boxes let the instructor simulate what happens away from the panel (cockpit, doors, lavatory, passengers).
SIMULATORPart of the app only (license, demo, settings, simulator options). Not on the aircraft.
NOT INCLUDEDA real A320 function that the simulator does not reproduce (Section 06).

Important limits

SECONDARY SOURCESOfficial Airbus manuals (FCOM, CCOM, AMM) are restricted to operators. This report relies on public technical notes, training material and Airbus publications. Every A320 is configured by its airline through the CAM (Cabin Assignment Module), so layouts, lighting scenarios, announcements, access codes and levels can differ from aircraft to aircraft. Always follow your airline's cabin crew manual.

Source numbers in square brackets, for example [4], refer to the list in Section 08.

Conclusion. Every FAP page, hard key and card slot of the simulator exists on the real A320 CIDS FAP, and the core behaviour of each one matches the sources: the ten hard keys, the colour code, BRT / DIM 1 / DIM 2, ±2.5 °C temperature fine adjustment, 200 L water and 170 L waste, automatic smoke page with SMOKE RESET and automatic clearing, guarded EVAC CMD with the CAPT / CAPT & PURS selector, CIDS 1+2 FAULT, and the default access codes 318 and 813. Version 4.0 adds the passenger signs with their chime, the CAUT key, lavatory calls, cockpit PA priority, emergency lighting in ARM, the full low cabin pressure and CIDS loss behaviour, layout selection on ground only, and the PRAM / CAM / OBRM card change.

The SIMPLIFIED items are mainly airline-configured content (announcement list, music channels, number of lighting scenarios, level values) where the simulator shows one typical configuration. The functions listed as NOT INCLUDED are maintenance or optional features that a cabin crew trainee does not normally use.

The simulator is a classroom familiarization tool. It is not a certified training device (see the disclaimer in Section 07).
