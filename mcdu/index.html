Cold & dark to shutdown

Overhead panel with BAT 1 / 2, EXT PWR, APU MASTER SW, START and BLEED, the three ADIRS selectors with ON BATT and ALIGN lights, and the engine MODE and MASTER switches. Align the IRS on IRS INIT, start the engines, fly, then shut down.

Departure & arrival

DEPARTURE and ARRIVAL pages with real runways and fictional training SIDs, STARs, ILS and RNAV approaches; airways, holds, offsets, FIX INFO, step climbs, wind pages and the SEC F-PLN.

MCDU like the aircraft

14-line display with the Airbus colour code, line select keys, scratchpad and CLR logic, and real messages such as NOT ALLOWED and FORMAT ERROR.

Navigation Display

Mode and range selectors as on the EFIS panel: ROSE ILS / VOR / NAV, ARC and PLAN, 10 to 320 NM, CSTR / WPT / VOR.D / NDB / ARPT, and the temporary flight plan in yellow.

Weather radar

MultiScan panel laid out as in the cockpit: GAIN and TILT knobs, the MODE selector (WX, WX+T, TURB, MAP) and the SYS, MULTISCAN, GCS and PWS switches. A high tilt overshoots the storm; a low tilt paints the ground.

TRAINER

Press FLY to fly your flight plan from Manila (RPLL) to Cebu (RPVM) at ×1, ×4 or ×16 speed. Waypoints sequence on the F-PLN page, a discontinuity stops managed navigation, and RESET brings the aircraft back to the gate. Dashed boxes mark controls that exist only in the trainer.

FAULTS lets the instructor fail a system: FM1 or FM2 (single FMGC, PLEASE WAIT after the reset), IND, MCDU FAIL, GPS (GPS PRIMARY LOST, NAV ACCUR DOWNGRAD on the approach), weather radar system 1 or 2, VOR 1 and ILS.

MCDU PAGES

The pages a student uses first

White is for titles and prompts, blue for data you can change, green for active data, amber boxes for required entries, magenta for constraints and yellow for a temporary flight plan.

INIT A / B

FROM/TO, company route, ALTN, flight number, cost index, CRZ FL, weights and fuel.

F-PLN

Insert and clear waypoints, discontinuities, speed and altitude constraints, (T/C) and (T/D).

LAT REV / VERT REV

NEXT WPT and NEW DEST with a yellow TMPY plan: *ERASE or INSERT*.

DIR TO

Direct to any point from the present position, then INSERT.

PERF

TAKE OFF V1 / VR / V2 with the disagree check, FLAPS/THS, FLEX, then CLB, CRZ, DES and APPR.

PROG

Cruise level, optimum and maximum level, bearing and distance to any point.

RAD NAV

VOR, ILS and ADF by identifier or frequency, with course.

FUEL PRED / DATA

Fuel at destination and alternate, extra fuel, position monitor, aircraft status.

DEPARTURE / ARRIVAL

Runway, SID and transition; approach, STAR and VIA; yellow TMPY, INSERT, missed approach after the destination.

IRS INIT / WIND / SEC

ALIGN ON REF and CONFIRM ALIGN, CLIMB / CRUISE / DESCENT WIND with WIND REQUEST, INIT REQUEST, SEC F-PLN.

HOLD / OFFSET / AIRWAYS

Holding pattern with IMM EXIT, lateral offset, airway VIA / TO, FIX INFO, LL XING, PLACE/BRG/DIST waypoints.

MCDU MENU / CFDS

Maintenance menu: leg reports and AVIONICS STATUS from the trainer faults, SYSTEM REPORT / TEST, CIDS 1 and 2 reports, LRU IDENT, GROUND SCANNING, SYSTEM CONFIG, OP TESTS and system tests.

License code required

The first time the app opens, enter your license code (for example MCDU-k7Rm9Qx2), exactly as given: capital and small letters matter. Activation needs an internet connection once. Each code works on one device only. A320 FAP codes do not work in this app. Ask your instructor for a code.

New device? On the old one tap ⚙ Settings, then Deactivate & Transfer License, then enter the same code on the new one.

No code yet? Try the free demo with code MCDU-DEMO (no internet needed): the INIT and F-PLN pages and the Navigation Display.
